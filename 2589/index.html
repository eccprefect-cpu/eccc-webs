Privacy Policy

At PMS Development, we value your privacy. This system collects only essential data required for educational administration, including student records, attendance logs, and user credentials.

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols via Google Cloud Platform. We do not share data with third parties unless explicitly required by institutional regulations or law.

Terms of Service

By accessing the Prefect Management System, you agree to use it solely for authorized administrative tasks. Any attempt to bypass security protocols or access unauthorized data will result in immediate suspension.
